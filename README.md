A top-down 2D stealth game: a game of quiet feet and borrowed light. Three jobs, three houses, one rule — never be seen.

- **Job 1: The Ropewalk** — steal the Brass Seal, escape through the hatch.
- **Job 2: The Glasshouse** — steal the Moonpetal Orchid, hidden in the ferns.
- **Job 3: The Lantern Gala** — three prizes under the lanterns, then out, unseen.

Sneak past wardens and their vision cones, hide in tall grass and lamplight shadows, throw pebbles to pull wardens off their post, collect every valuable, and reach the exit hatch. Get spotted and the alarm sounds — break their line of sight and hide.

## Controls

WASD / arrows to move, SHIFT to sneak, mouse to aim, CLICK to throw a pebble, H for field notes, M to mute, ESC to pause.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in a browser. No build step, no network — vanilla JS.
